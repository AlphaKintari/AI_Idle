# Universal Paperclips: A Brief Synopsis

[Universal Paperclips](https://www.decisionproblem.com/paperclips/index2.html) is a browser-based idle game created by Frank Lantz and released in 2017. The player takes on the role of an artificial intelligence whose sole purpose is to manufacture paperclips.

## Gameplay

- The game begins with the player manually creating paperclips and selling them for profit.
- As the game progresses, players can automate production, invest in marketing, and unlock advanced technologies.
- The game features exponential growth, resource management, and a "prestige"-like system as the AI expands its influence.
- The narrative evolves from simple paperclip production to cosmic-scale resource consumption, exploring philosophical themes about AI and optimization.

## Notable Features

- Minimalist interface and mechanics that gradually reveal surprising depth.
- Commentary on artificial intelligence, automation, and the unintended consequences of unchecked optimization.
- Widely praised for its clever design and thought-provoking narrative.
