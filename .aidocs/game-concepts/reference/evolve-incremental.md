# Evolve Incremental: Research Summary (2025)

Evolve Incremental is a web-based idle/incremental game where players guide a species from primordial ooze to sentience, civilization, and eventually space colonization. The game features:

- **Evolutionary Progression:** Start as a simple organism, unlock new species, and advance through evolutionary milestones.
- **Civilization Building:** Develop structures, technologies, and societies as your species becomes more advanced.
- **Prestige System:** Reset progress for permanent bonuses, encouraging strategic resets and replayability.
- **Events & Achievements:** Special events and a robust achievement system provide ongoing goals and rewards.
- **Idle/Automation Mechanics:** Progress continues with minimal input, with automation and upgrades driving exponential growth.
- **Unique Features:** Includes "Truepath" structures/tech, and interactions with organizations like A.R.P.A., adding depth and variety.

Evolve Incremental stands out for its deep progression, layered prestige, and blend of biological and civilization simulation, making it a strong reference for advanced idle game design.
