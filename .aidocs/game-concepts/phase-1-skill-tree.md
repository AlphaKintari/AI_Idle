
# Phase 1 Skill Tree (Simplified)

## Default Skills (Always Enabled)

- **Computation**
- **Memory**



## Progression Path & Level Requirements

Each skill requires the previous skill to reach a specific level before it can be unlocked. As the player progresses, each new level is harder to achieve than the last, requiring more resources or time.

1. **Computation** (Level 1, default) + **Memory** (Level 1, default)
    ↓
2. **Learning** (Requires Computation & Memory at Level 2)
    ↓
3. **Optimization** (Requires Learning at Level 3)
    ↓
4. **Autonomy** (Requires Optimization at Level 4)
    ↓
5. **Networking** (Requires Autonomy at Level 5)
    ↓
6. **Communication** (Requires Networking at Level 6)
    ↓
7. **Security** (Requires Communication at Level 7)

> _Note: Each level is progressively harder to achieve, making higher skills increasingly challenging to unlock._

---


## Advanced Skills (Require Multiple Prerequisites & Levels)

- **Creativity**: Requires **Learning** (Level 2) & **Communication** (Level 6)
- **Emotional Intelligence**: Requires **Communication** (Level 6) & **Memory** (Level 1)
- **Self-Repair**: Requires **Security** (Level 7) & **Optimization** (Level 3)
- **Foresight**: Requires **Learning** (Level 2), **Autonomy** (Level 4), & **Memory** (Level 1)

**Summary:**
- Start with Computation and Memory.
- Progress linearly through the core skills.
- Unlock advanced skills by meeting multiple prerequisites.
- This structure encourages strategic planning and meaningful choices as the AI evolves.
